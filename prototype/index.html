How to Set Up Your Online Account with NICE test

Welcome to NICE test, your trusted telecommunication provider. Setting up your online account is quick and easy. Follow these simple instructions to get started and access your account anytime, anywhere.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Visit our official website at www.nicetest.com and click "Sign Up" or "Create Account" in the top right corner.
  2. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number associated with your NICE test service.
  3. Create a strong password with at least 8 characters including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.
  4. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox.
  5. Log in and explore your account dashboard.

How to Cancel an Order with NICE test

We understand that plans change. If you need to cancel an order, NICE test makes the process straightforward. Follow the steps below to cancel your order quickly and receive confirmation.

Before You Begin — have ready:

  • Your order number or reference ID
  • The email address used to place the order
  • Your account login credentials

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Log in to your NICE test account at www.nicetest.com.
  2. Navigate to My Orders from the account dashboard.
  3. Locate the order and click View Details.
  4. Click Cancel Order. If greyed out, the order is already processed — contact support.
  5. Select a cancellation reason and confirm.
  6. A confirmation email will arrive within 5 minutes.

Need Help?

Contact our support team at 1-800-NICETEST or via live chat, available 24/7.
